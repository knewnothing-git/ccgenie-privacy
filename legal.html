Who we are

ccGenie is an independent credit card comparison and rewards-optimisation app published by dev yogi (Yogesh P. Choudhary), Pune, India. In this document "we", "us" and "the app" mean ccGenie.

We are not a bank, lender, broker or financial adviser. We do not issue credit cards, approve applications, or make lending decisions. Nothing in the app is personalised financial advice.

How we use it

Your profile and recent transactions are used to generate card recommendations, reward projections and answers in the in-app advisor. To do this, that information is sent from your device to our backend server, which passes it to Google's Gemini API to generate the recommendation text.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build profiles for third parties.

If you would rather your financial details never leave your device, avoid the AI recommendation and advisor features. The rest of the app — the card directory, the rewards calculator and your saved transactions — works entirely on-device.

Storage & retention

Your profile and transactions are stored locally on your device in the app's own storage. We do not operate user accounts and we do not keep a copy of your profile on our servers. Information sent to our backend is used to fulfil that one request and is not stored by us afterwards; the third parties above retain data under their own policies.

Because the data lives on your device, uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also erase everything at any time from Profile → Delete account data inside the app.
