Delete your ccGenie data

Applies to the ccGenie Android app (com.ccgenie.app) · Last updated 6 September 2026

How to delete it

ccGenie has no user accounts. Your profile and transactions are stored on your own device, so you can delete everything yourself in a few seconds — you do not need to contact us or wait for a request to be processed.

  1. Open ccGenie and go to the Profile tab The person icon in the top-right of the app header.
  2. Tap "Delete account data" Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and confirm when prompted.
  3. Everything is erased immediately Your saved profile, your transactions and your session are removed from the device. Nothing is recoverable afterwards.

Uninstalling the app also deletes all of it. Because the data never leaves your device in stored form, removing ccGenie removes the data with it.

If you would rather we did it

If you cannot access the app, or you want confirmation in writing, email us and we will action the request and reply within 30 days. Please send from the address you used in the app, if you provided one.

devsideofmine@gmail.com

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happensRetention
Profile — name, email, address, income, credit score, goals Deleted from your device None. Not stored on our servers at any point
Transactions you added, uploaded or scanned Deleted from your device None. Not stored on our servers
App session and preferences Deleted from your device None
AI recommendation requests Nothing to delete — processed in transit only Not retained by us. Google's Gemini API handles it under its own terms
Subscription and purchase records Kept Held by RevenueCat and Google Play against an anonymous app user ID, and retained for accounting, tax and refund purposes. We cannot delete Google Play's transaction records; email us to have the RevenueCat record removed

To cancel an active subscription, use the Google Play Store — Menu → Subscriptions. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription, and cancelling a subscription does not delete your data.
